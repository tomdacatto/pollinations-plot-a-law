Plot a Law
Signed in with your Pollen

A science topic you can move, and check

Most "explain this topic" tools let a model write a page full of code and run it. Here the model writes no code. It describes a law as data: parameters, one or two equations, and three worked examples it calculated by hand. This page draws the equations with sliders using a small expression reader of its own, then runs the model's worked examples against its equations. A ✓ means the formula and the model's own arithmetic agree; a ✗ is shown, not hidden, and you can ask the model to repair it once. It cannot tell you the law is true: it catches a model contradicting itself.
